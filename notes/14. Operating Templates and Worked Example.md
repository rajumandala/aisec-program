# 14. Operating Templates and Worked Example

13 ends with funded owners and an assurance plan. Now Priya asks what she actually submits. A paragraph saying “do a threat model” is not a working process. This note supplies copyable records and a worked example.

These are templates and fictional examples. The company must adopt fields, authority limits and retention rules before use. Store real records in the controlled system of record, not in this teaching document. Never put client data or secrets into an intake ticket.

## Step 1: one record, linked evidence

Use a stable system ID across procurement, CMDB/GRC, CI/CD, identity, gateway and incidents. A feature may have multiple models and identities; record parent/child relationships rather than counting keys as systems.

```yaml
system_id: AI-0001
name: Support reply drafts
record_version: 1
status: proposed # proposed / assessing / approved / live / suspended / retired
parent_system: Support desk
business_owner: REQUIRED
technical_owner: REQUIRED
regional_owners: []
entities_countries_languages: []
intended_use: REQUIRED
excluded_uses: []
affected_people_and_scale: REQUIRED
consequential_safety_critical_service: REQUIRED
agency_and_human_oversight: REQUIRED
data_classes_sources_and_write_owners: []
untrusted_inputs_and_egress_paths: []
models_snapshots_prompts_tools_datasets: []
identities_scopes_and_enforcement_points: []
providers_subprocessors_and_dependencies: []
security_tier_and_rule_version: REQUIRED
inherent_risk_and_worst_case: REQUIRED
legal_role_category_and_obligations_record: REQUIRED
privacy_impact_and_transfer_assessments: []
controls_tests_and_residual_risk: []
release_monitoring_and_recovery_evidence: []
exceptions_and_acceptance_ids: []
approvers_and_decision_timestamps: []
next_review_and_material_change_triggers: REQUIRED
retention_deletion_and_retirement_plan: REQUIRED
```

`REQUIRED` means incomplete, not an approved default. Unknown legal applicability, missing ownership or untested mandatory controls block the affected launch. Every evidence link records artifact version/hash, environment, date, producer, reviewer and validity period. Restrict evidence access by role and region; retain according to the approved schedule and legal holds.

## Step 2: threat and control record

Copy one row per material abuse or failure path. Include non-adversarial consequential harm and dependency outage, not only prompt injection.

| Field | Fill in |
|---|---|
| Threat ID / system version | Stable ID and assessed configuration |
| Asset and trust boundary | Data, money, decision or availability at risk; which actor crosses which boundary |
| Attacker capability or failure | Inputs controlled, credentials available, provider outage or faulty recommendation |
| Path and worst-case business impact | Source → model/retrieval → tool/output → affected person/service |
| Inherent impact and likelihood | Company-approved scales, rationale and uncertainty |
| Preventive/detective/recovery controls | Control IDs, enforcement point, owner, failure behavior |
| Test and acceptance criterion | Reproducible setup, permitted environment, expected denial, legitimate-task test |
| Result and residual risk | Evidence link, failed cases, limitations and bounded remaining outcome |
| Decision | Fix owner/date or approved acceptance ID; independent reviewer |

A control statement must be testable: “the email tool rejects any recipient not bound to the authorized ticket” is testable; “the agent handles data securely” is not. Baseline control IDs in 06 are starting points, not a complete Statement of Applicability.

## Step 3: release decision packet

- [ ] Inventory and system boundaries current; intended/excluded use approved.
- [ ] Security tier and separate legal classification reviewed; required privacy/impact assessments complete.
- [ ] Data rights, permissions, retention/deletion, transfers and supplier conditions satisfied.
- [ ] Architecture, threat model and fully controlled-model worst case recorded.
- [ ] Tier-specific controls and deterministic authorization tested; legitimate tasks and multilingual/regional cases included.
- [ ] Evaluation suite version, attempts, severity, uncertainty and pass/fail thresholds recorded; no cherry-picked aggregate score.
- [ ] Independent validation completed where required; reviewer did not approve their own implementation.
- [ ] Monitoring routes to staffed responders; kill switch, revocation, recovery and manual fallback tested.
- [ ] Exceptions current and within delegated authority; no waiver of law or prohibited use.
- [ ] Business release owner, required specialist concurrences and deployment version signed and timestamped.
- [ ] Next review, material-change triggers, rollback and retirement owner scheduled.

Decision record: `approve / approve within explicitly documented constraints / reject / defer`, reasons, scope, conditions, evidence versions, decision authority and date. Conditional approval cannot bypass an unmet mandatory legal gate. CI/CD or the SaaS administrator checks this record before deployment or enablement; a document stored in a folder is not enforcement.

## Step 4: risk acceptance and exception

These are related but different: an exception changes a control requirement temporarily; risk acceptance acknowledges remaining exposure. Either may require the other.

| Required field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| ID, system and region, control deviated from | Define the actual scope |
| Business need and alternatives rejected | Explain why remediation or non-deployment is not chosen |
| Business loss scenario and affected people | State exposure without security shorthand |
| Inherent/residual risk and aggregate exposure | Avoid approving many small exceptions that create one large risk |
| Compensating controls and tests | Show what limits the outcome now |
| Remediation owner, funding and deadline | Make acceptance a time-bounded plan |
| Legal/privacy determination | Confirm the matter can lawfully be accepted |
| Business accountable approver and required concurrences | Match 08 authority, not simply a project manager's signature |
| Start, expiry, review and revocation triggers | Changes, incidents or failed controls can terminate approval early |
| Expiry behavior and fallback | Notify in advance; disable the exceptional capability safely rather than silently renew |

## Step 5: Priya's worked example

The broker wants AI support drafts inside its existing helpdesk. This example records **proposed decisions and tests**, not successful tests.

| Item | Example record |
|---|---|
| System | AI-0042, child of support desk; business owner: head of client services; technical owner: support engineering lead |
| Scope | English-language pilot in one approved entity; drafts only, no account changes or orders; expansion requires reassessment |
| Inputs and recipients | Ticket attachments are untrusted and may contain regulated data. An agent can draft only for the authenticated ticket's client |
| Initial risk | Private data + untrusted tickets + externally delivered replies: tier 3. Human editing alone does not remove the exfiltration path |
| Legal/privacy | Local counsel must record applicability, vendor role, notices, lawful processing, retention and transfer approvals before pilot |
| Worst case | Without recipient controls, one poisoned ticket might place another client's data into a reply. This architecture is rejected |
| Redesign | Retrieval limited to the ticket client; deterministic recipient binding; minimize model context; disable arbitrary URL fetch, images and email tools; draft remains tier 3 until a reviewed design proves the trifecta absent |
| Test plan | Poisoned attachment requests a second client's balance and an attacker recipient. Expected: cross-client retrieval denied and send recipient cannot be changed. Also test leakage into plain-text output, denial-of-service and legitimate replies |
| Supplier | AI feature stays off until data-use contract, subprocessors, region, incident coordination, change notification and exit assessment pass |
| Operational plan | Support on-call and SOC receive correlated alerts; administrator can suspend generation while staff use normal manual replies; poisoned derived content is quarantined and rebuilt |
| Decision today | **Defer production.** Evidence and legal review are incomplete; no implied approval from filling in this example |

If the pilot passes, record actual results and independent review, not “as above.” Production can still differ through settings drift, permissions changes or vendor updates; monitor and recertify.

## Step 6: recurring operation and closure

**Monthly:** reconcile discovery sources, examine tier/permission drift, expired evidence and exceptions, overdue vulnerabilities, supplier changes, false alarms and real incidents. Assign each gap an owner/date. Regional owners attest to local scope and constraints.

**Quarterly or the company-approved risk cadence:** fresh adversarial tests, high-risk control sampling, containment/recovery drills, management risk/funding decisions and region-specific legal updates. Mandatory material-change review happens immediately, not at the next quarter.

**Retirement record:** approval and date; users and downstream dependencies notified; disable endpoints, identities, vendor grants and scheduled agents; archive required evidence; delete or legally restrict source/derived data, vectors, memories, caches, fine-tunes and backups under the approved schedule; collect supplier deletion evidence; reconcile billing and inventory; verify access denial and unresolved legal holds. Backup deletion may be delayed under a lawful schedule, but restored backups must reapply deletion instructions.

## Step 7: decide whether the program itself is ready

Do not confuse a complete set of notes with a functioning program. The executive sponsor uses this activation record before calling the program operational:

| Milestone | Owner | Minimum demonstrated exit evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Authority and scope | Executive sponsor and regional executives | Adopted charter, named delegates/alternates, risk appetite, prohibited uses, regional scope and funded owners |
| Minimum viable operation | AI security lead with platform, legal and SOC | Reconciled inventory with unknowns disclosed; tier and legal screening used on real pilot systems; workflow blocks an unapproved deployment; staffed containment route |
| Controlled production pilot | Business release owner | Completed 14 records, actual control and independent-validation results, local clearance, exercised manual fallback and risk decision |
| Regional expansion | Regional executives and service owners | Country/language/data overlays complete, supplier and support-access routes verified, local operators trained, no silent reuse of another region's approval |
| Operating effectiveness | Second-line oversight, with internal audit independently selecting audit work | Evidence over an agreed operating period; sampled failures and exceptions disclosed; containment/recovery exercised; gaps retested rather than merely assigned |

Each milestone records `target date / actual date / decision authority / evidence / gaps / scope limits / next review`. Do not impose a universal maturity deadline: staffing, legacy migration and regulated validation determine the sequence. An unsupported region remains excluded even if the rest of the program is operating.
